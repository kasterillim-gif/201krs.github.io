Inclusive Futures

Accessible and Assistive Emerging Technology Research

Lab Mission

The Inclusive Futures Lab researches how assistive and emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence (AI), can safely and ethically support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) and the people who support them, from birth to death.

The lab develops technology training, cybersecurity awareness, and accessible technology practices for individuals with IDD, their families, teachers, direct support professionals, clinicians, and service providers.

Foundational Work

The lab connects disability services research with emerging technology across the whole lifespan, including early intervention, school years, the transition to adulthood, employment and day services, and aging. Our core focus areas are:

  • Assistive and Accessible Technology Matching tools such as communication apps and AAC to each person's needs and abilities.
  • Cybersecurity Digital Literacy Helping individuals with IDD and their supporters protect personal information and avoid scams.
  • Ethical AI Responsible, transparent, and person-centered use of AI in support, education, and care.
  • Emerging Technology Evaluating tools such as VR/AR, smart glasses, and wearables for people with IDD.

Research Focus Areas

  • Technology training for supporters Develop practical training for direct support professionals, teachers, families, and other staff who support individuals with IDD, and study how they transfer what they learn to the people they support.
  • Safe technology use across the lifespan Design digital literacy and cybersecurity guidance suited to each life stage, from children to older adults with IDD.
  • Ethical AI in disability services Identify guidelines for privacy, consent, and bias when AI is used with people with IDD.

Why It Matters

People with IDD and the staff and families who support them often have the least access to technology training, yet face real risks such as scams, loss of privacy, and misuse of AI. The Inclusive Futures Lab builds practical, accessible training so technology can improve independence, communication, and quality of life from birth to death.

Collaboration (CEHC and Partners)

UAlbany Research Labs The lab welcomes collaborations to develop and test technology training for direct support professionals, teachers, and families; evaluate assistive and accessible technologies; study how supporters transfer training into everyday practice; and examine privacy, consent, and cybersecurity for people with IDD.

Disability Service Organizations and Schools Pilot training and technology with the people they support and their staff.

Students The lab is open to undergraduate and graduate students interested in assistive technology, accessibility, cybersecurity, and ethical AI. No prior research experience is required.

Potential Impact on Education

  • Digital Habit Building Teach safe habits such as password and personal information security and scam spotting.
  • Mainstream Awareness Raise awareness of accessible and assistive technologies, including communication apps.
  • Professional Readiness Prepare future support professionals and educators to use technology and AI responsibly.
  • Lifespan Frameworks Build training that works for staff and families at every stage of a person's life.
  • Community Partnerships Foster partnerships between higher education and organizations that serve people with IDD.
